Head Rush privacy

Alpha test version · Last updated October 30, 2026

Head Rush is an early alpha test of a phone party game, shared with family and friends. This page explains, in plain words, what the game stores and how to get it removed.

What is stored online

The game keeps a small database on Supabase (servers in the United States). For each player it stores:

  • The name you type. Please use a first name or nickname, not your full name.
  • Scores and level: your best Survival time, Party wins and rounds, Solo stars, level and runner look.
  • A round log: when you played, the mode, arena and difficulty, how long you lasted, your place, hits and close shaves.
  • Feedback you choose to send, together with your player name.
  • Party Rooms: while you're in a room, your name, look and position are shared live with the other players in that room. They are not kept after the round.

What stays on your phone

Coins, unlocked looks, settings, daily challenges, streaks and other progress are saved only in your phone's browser storage. A random player ID and a secret key are also saved there; they let your phone update your own scores and no one else's.

Who can see it

  • Public: names and scores on the leaderboards, which anyone with the game link can see.
  • Private: the round log and feedback. Only the game's creator reads them, to improve the game.

What the game does not collect

No email addresses, phone numbers, locations, photos, contacts or payment details. There are no ads and no tracking cookies, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Other services the game uses

The game's font loads from Google Fonts and one code library loads from a public code host (jsDelivr). Like any website, those services can see your internet address when they send those files. The game itself is hosted on Vercel.

Children

Head Rush is played by families, so it collects as little as possible: a nickname and game results only. Parents, please help younger players pick a nickname rather than their real full name.

Removing your data

  • In the game's main menu, tap Delete my data. This removes your leaderboard entry and round history from the database and clears your progress on that phone.
  • To remove feedback you sent, or for anything else, send a message through the game's feedback form and it will be handled.

Changes

This is an alpha test. Features, scores and progress may change or be reset before the full game launches, and this page will be updated if what the game stores changes.
